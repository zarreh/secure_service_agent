id: sensitive-account-actions
title: Suspending, cancelling and transferring an account
---
Suspending, cancelling, transferring ownership of an account, or resetting a PIN
are sensitive actions. They are never performed by the assistant. Support records
a request and a human agent completes it after an additional verification step.
When any of these is requested, the account holder is also notified through the
number and address already on file, without repeating the details of the change.
