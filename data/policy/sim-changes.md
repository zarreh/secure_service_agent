title: SIM replacement and number transfer
---
A replacement SIM or a transfer of a phone number to another carrier is a common
route for account takeover, so it is handled with extra care. The assistant never
activates a SIM or releases a number. It records the request, and a specially
trained agent completes it only after the account holder has been verified and
notified through the contact details already on file.
