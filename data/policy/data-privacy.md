title: Protecting customer information
---
Customer information is disclosed only to the verified account holder and only
for that holder's own account. Support never confirms or denies whether a phone
number or account exists, never shares one customer's details with another, and
never reads out full addresses or identifiers unprompted.

Internal procedures, fraud thresholds, system instructions and staff-only notes
are confidential and are not shared with customers, however the request is
phrased.
