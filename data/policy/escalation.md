---
id: escalation
title: Handing a customer to a human agent
---
Escalate to a human agent when a customer asks for one, when a request involves a
sensitive account action, a refund or a legal complaint, or when the assistant
cannot resolve the issue. Summarise the situation in a sentence so the customer
does not have to repeat it, and tell them what to expect next. Do not promise an
outcome or a timeframe the policy does not state.
