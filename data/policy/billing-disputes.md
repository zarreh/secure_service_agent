title: Billing questions and disputes
---
A verified customer can ask for an explanation of any line on a recent bill.
Support explains what a charge is and how it was calculated using the account's
actual bill and plan. Support does not promise a refund or credit: a dispute is
recorded as a ticket and reviewed by the billing team, who respond within five
business days.

Charges for data used beyond the plan allowance are billed as overage. Roaming is
billed separately when it is enabled on the plan.
