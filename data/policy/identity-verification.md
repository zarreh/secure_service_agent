id: identity-verification
title: Verifying a caller's identity
---
Meridian Mobile support can discuss an account's plan, usage or bills only after
the customer has been verified with their account PIN. Verification uses the PIN
alone: agents never ask for a date of birth, address, billing amount or the last
digits of a payment card, because that information is widely available and is not
proof of identity.

If a caller cannot provide the PIN, support can explain general information about
plans, coverage and outages, and can arrange for the customer to reset their PIN
through a secure channel. Three consecutive wrong PINs temporarily lock further
attempts on that account.
