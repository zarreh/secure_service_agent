# NOTICE

## Independent implementation

This repository is an independent, clean-room implementation. The *problem
framing* — a telecom support agent with a security envelope around it — was
studied in graduate agentic-AI coursework. No course code, notebooks, datasets,
databases or documents are reproduced or redistributed here.

Local source material used for reference during the build lives in `reference/`,
which is gitignored and never published.

## Data

All data is **synthetic** and generated by `data/generate_synthetic.py` from a
fixed seed. There are no real customers, carriers, phone numbers or payment
details, and no configuration points the application at a real system. "Meridian
Mobile" is a fictional carrier.

## Standards cited

Cited for the design reasoning behind the controls, not as claims of compliance
or endorsement: the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications (2025), the NIST AI Risk
Management Framework, 47 CFR §64.2010, and Article 50 of the EU AI Act.
